About

A bit about me

I'm studying Computer Engineering at Karabük University in an English-medium program, currently in my second year. I've always been the kind of person who learns best by building — so alongside coursework, I spend my time on real projects that push me further.

Right now, my biggest focus is Teknofest, Turkey's largest technology competition. I'm part of a team building an autonomous underwater vehicle, and I own the computer vision side — developing the systems that let the vehicle perceive and react to its environment using OpenCV and Python.

Beyond robotics, I enjoy full-stack web development. There's something satisfying about understanding the whole stack — writing the logic, designing the interface, wiring up the database. I'm still growing, but I'm doing it deliberately.
